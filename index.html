Before You Come In

Good Questions.

Quick answers for first-time visitors, longtime collectors, and the curious.

Do I need to know comics to come in?

Not at all. Tell us what you like — movies, games, spooky stories, big feelings — and we can point you toward a first issue.

Do you carry back issues?

Yes. The long boxes are part of the fun. Ask at the counter if you are looking for a particular character, run, or era.

Can you help me choose a graphic novel?

Absolutely. Let us know how much time you have and what kind of story you want. We will find a good match without spoiling the ending.

Do you buy collections?

We look at comics, graphic novels, manga, and collectibles. Email or call ahead so we can make time to talk through what you have.

When do new comics arrive?

New books typically land on Wednesday. Follow Salem Comics on social for the latest arrivals and shop updates.
